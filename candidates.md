## Judge brief criteria (in order — first difference decides)

1. **C1 — Single word describing a boss**: the sort of word a person would actually use about the one they report to.
2. **C2 — Funny on its own**: the kind of word a colleague repeats at lunch because it lands.
3. **C3 — Rings true**: catches a real habit of bosses the entrant has clearly met.
4. **C4 — Fresh**: not the first word anyone would reach for; surprise earns a laugh, not a shrug.
5. **C5 — Travels**: works for a boss in a kitchen, a warehouse or an office without the industry needing to be explained.

Also: a word that needs a caption to be funny does less than one that stands alone. Shock for its own sake does not count.
